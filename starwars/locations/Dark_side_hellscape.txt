A dark side hellscape was located in the galaxy. Its location was known to the former Sith Lord Maul and Lady Qi'ra, the leader of Crimson Dawn,[1] but was unknown to the Sith Lords Darth Sidious and Darth Vader.[2] Prior to his defeat at the hands of the Jedi Order, the Sith Lord Darth Momin visited the hellscape.[3] Momin's Fermata Cage[4] was kept stored in the hellscape. Thousands of years later,[1] between 3 ABY and 4 ABY,[5] the Knights of Ren and the Archivist visited the hellscape to recover the Fermata Cage at the request of Qi'ra.[1]
