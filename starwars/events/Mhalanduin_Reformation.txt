The Mhalanduin Reformation was an event[2] that shared its name with the Mhalanduin system of the Mid Rim's Oktos sector.[3] At some point by 0 BBY,[1] a dispute involving two families had arisen over the reformation.[2] Sometime between 19 BBY[4] and 0 BBY, officer Conan Antonio Motti of the Galactic Empire's Imperial Navy ended the dispute by destroying the top ranks of both involved families and subsequently appointing as regent a general that he himself had picked.[2]
Behind the scenes
The Mhalanduin Reformation was originally meant to be introduced in the 2012 reference book The Essential Guide to Warfare by Jason Fry and Paul R. Urquhart; however, the section that would have mentioned it was cut before the book's release. The information regarding the event was later released in 2014 as part of Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Warfare Author's Cut, a series of articles published on the StarWars.com Blog by Fry and[2] Del Rey editor[5] Erich Schoeneweiss containing various cut content from the book.[2]
