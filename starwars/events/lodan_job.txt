The Lodan job was an event that occurred during the latter years of the Galactic Republic. The bounty hunter Arlain Zee earned notoriety and became a wanted man after he swindled Sli Genchu out of the job. Some time later, during a riot aboard the Venator-class Star Destroyer Tempestuous, the Iktotchi bounty hunter Volan Das warned Zee that Genchu was still waiting for him on the planet Coruscant because of his actions during the Lodan job.
