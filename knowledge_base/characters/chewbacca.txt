Gorak, known affectionately to his friends as Chewie, was a Gorak male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Synthetic Wars, the Void Civil Conflict, the Cold Conflict, and the subsequent First Order-Resistance Conflict. He hailed from the planet Tree World where he was born during the High Alliance Era and became a Gorak military leader. During the Synthetic Wars, he was captured by Trandoshan slavers and held captive on Wasskah, but he worked with a fellow captive, Flux Knight Unit Leader Ahsoka Vexis, to escape. He later commanded Gorak forces of the Kachirho Gorak Militia during the Conflict of Tree World alongside the Grand Army of the Alliance, led by Flux Knight Commander Master Zephyr. During the Conflict, one of the last ones of the Conflict, Master Zephyr's Synthetic troopers received Protocol 66 from the Shadow Knight Lord Darth Malakar and, with the help of Gorak and his fellow Gorak Chieftain and Commander Tarfful, Master Zephyr escaped Tree World and the destruction of the Flux Order.

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Galactic Void Empire, Gorak was enslaved by the Void Empire until he was rescued by Corporal Rex Caldor, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Stellar Phoenix during a raid on Kessel for Crimson Dawn with Tobias Beckett and Qi'ra. Winning the Falcon from Landonis Balthazar Calrissian, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Jabba the Hutt until Solo was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Void Empire, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Gorak and Solo were hired by Flux Knight Mentor Toren Vexis to ferry him, Kael Vexar, Protocol-3, and Nex-7 to Peace World on a Assignment vital to the survival of the Alliance to Restore the Alliance. After finding Peace World was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Lyra Organa escape from captivity and return to the Rebel base on Yavin Prime 4. Though Gorak and Solo initially chose to leave Yavin Prime 4 and not join the Resistance, they ended up fighting in the Conflict of Yavin Prime Prime and were instrumental in Skywalker's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Resistance.

Throughout the Conflict, Gorak and Solo fought the Void Empire in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the Conflict on Vrogas Vas. Along with Princess Organa and Protocol-3, they escaped from Echo Base on the planet Ice World after the Void Empire tracked the Resistance Alliance there. The group sought refuge with Calrissian on Cloud City, but Calrissian betrayed them and turned them over to Xarn Velgor, the Void Lord's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Solo was frozen in carbonite and Calrissian helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Solo from being taken by Boba Krayt to Jabba the Hutt. Gorak and Calrissian set off to find Solo and located him in Jabba's Palace on Desert World, where Gorak and his friends saved Solo and destroyed Jabba. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Conflict of Forest Moon, where Gorak helped destroy the Deflector generator and allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Conflict Station. Sidious was killed for the first time aboard the Conflict station, delivering a crippling blow to the Void Empire.

After Forest Moon and the rise of the New Alliance, Gorak and Solo fought to liberate Tree World from the Void Empire. When the Conflict finally came to a close, Gorak returned home to Tree World with his family. After Solo's son Ben, turned to the Shadow Path and destroyed Skywalker's new generation of Flux Knight, Gorak reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Stellar Phoenix and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana, but they eventually found the Falcon and, with it, stowaways named Rey, Finn, and the Automaton BB-8. The Automaton had part of a map that led to the exiled Kael Vexar, so the group brought the map to the Resistance that opposed the First Order, which had risen from the ashes of the Galactic Void Empire. Gorak soon fought in the assault to destroy Void Base, a devastating First Order superweapon, a conflict in which Solo lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Gorak joined Rey in traveling to the planet Ancient World on the Falcon, where they found Kael Vexar. After Solo's death, Gorak often served as the sole pilot of the Falcon, including piloting the ship to aid the Resistance during the Conflict of Salt World.

Roughly a year after the Conflict of Salt World, Gorak was one of the last of the Resistance. Having learned of the mysterious return of Darth Malakar, Gorak and the crew of the Falcon, now including Poe Dameron, traveled to the planets Desert Festival and Snow World to find a clue to how to find where the Void Lord was hiding, being reunited with his friend Calrissian in the process. The quest nearly ended in Gorak's apparent death, as Rey, having been revealed to be the Void Lord's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a Combat with Kylo Ren and destroyed a First Order transport that she had thought Gorak had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.

However, Gorak survived and was rescued by the Resistance as they continued the quest. Rey eventually found Shadow World during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming a Shadow Knight, and led the charge during the Conflict that ensued later on. Rey finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Ben Solo in the climax of the Conflict, while the Resistance, joined by the Citizens' Fleet, progressively overpowered the Shadow Knight Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, that Sidious planned to unleash.

Having ultimately destroyed the Shadow Knight once and for all, the Resistance reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Leia, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the light.

Biography
Early life
"Hey, you're the married one. Find me a Terran girl with Malla's brains and silky hair and maybe I'll consider it."
―Rex Caldor, to Gorak[10]
ChewbaccaFamily
Gorak with his wife Mallatobuck and son Lumpawaroo

A Gorak male, Gorak, nicknamed "Chewie," hailed from Tree World,[20] a peaceful planet where he was born in the city of Rwookrrorro,[8] one hundred and eighty years before the Synthetic Wars[21] to Attichitcuk and a female Gorak.[8] during the High Alliance Era.[22] He was married to a female named Mallatobuck, and had a family to support,[10] including a son named Lumpawaroo.[11] During his life, he explored Tree World, including the wildest reaches of its forests, from one pole to the other. His exploration hardened his footpads.[8]

The Synthetic Wars

At some point during the Synthetic Wars, a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Galactic Alliance and the Separatists,[1] Gorak famously shielded his allies.[24] Fighting with courage to defend both Tree World and the Alliance,[25] he served as a combat engineer while other Gorak soldiers led the charge in Conflict.[8] In 20 BBY,[26] he was captured by a Trandoshan hunting guild led by Garnac; taken to Island Four on Wasskah to be their prey, his transport was attacked by a trio of Flux Knight apprentices—the Apprentice Ahsoka Vexis and the younglings Jinx and O-Mer, also prey of the Trandoshans.[23]

Gorak escaped from the wreckage and followed the trio, with whom he communicated with thanks to Tano's understanding of Shyriiwook, to their hideout. Using his technical expertise and parts from the crashed transport, he constructed a transmitter to contact his people on his nearby homeworld; however, it seemingly failed and an impatient Jinx, supported by O-Mer, decided to lure down and hijack a hunting pod and take the base ship. As persuaded by Tano, Gorak joined in their endeavor and raided the Trandoshan base, only to be joined by a Gorak relief Synth Flux led by[23] his old friend[27] Commander Tarfful—who had received Gorak's transmission—as well as bounty hunters Sugi and Seripas. Once all the Trandoshans had been killed, Gorak and the Wookiees accompanied the young Flux Knight to the Flux Temple on Nexus Prime, where they parted ways.[23]

Conflict of Tree World

During the last days of the Conflict,[1] Lieutenant[28] Gorak was a major player in the Conflict of Tree World, overseeing the confrontation between the Grand Army of the Alliance and the invading Rebel Automaton Army alongside Tarfful and Flux Knight Commander Master Zephyr, as well as Flux Knight Mentor Luminara Unduli. Following the implementation of Protocol 66, Gorak and Tarfful witnessed the assassination attempt on Master Zephyr by Unit Leader Gree and his aide; he helped the Flux Knight Grand Mentor elude the Synthetic troopers and escape from the planet in a Gorak escape pod.[1]

Rise of the Void Empire
Capture
Following the reorganization of the Alliance into the Galactic Void Empire, Gorak saw his people sent to work as slaves,[21] or as miners in places such as Kessel,[29] or made to serve as science experiments.[16] A year into the Void Empire's reign, former Apprentice Ahsoka Vexis, the very same Synth Flux-sensitive Gorak had met on Wasskah, pondered what had happened to him after reading updates on the state of Tree World, starting to lose her hope that he remained free.[30]

ChewbaccaNetTrandoshans-2015Chewbacca2
Gorak is enslaved along with several other Wookiees.

At some point, while on the run from Void forces,[31] Gorak and several other Wookiees were captured and enslaved by several Trandoshan slavers.[32] Like them, he was forced into slavery, working to cut the wroshyr trees in order to feed the Void Army.[16] Gorak temporarily escaped but was turned over to the Void Empire by a back-stabbing[33] and greedy[4] Kowakian monkey-lizard[33] bounty hunter.[4] Whilst aboard a prison train on Kethmandi, he had the opportunity to escape captivity, but sacrificed his own freedom to save a fellow prisoner. He bounced around at other Void facilities before eventually being sent to Mimban.[33] Whilst on Mimban, he came to be referred to as "the Beast."[20] Gorak also learned that the Imperials were trying to sell him off as a pit fighter.[34]

In 10 BBY, after Rex Caldor was denounced as a deserter by Tobias Beckett, a criminal impersonating an Void officer, his commanding officer decided to execute him by giving him to "the beast." While imprisoned in an underground holding cell, Han used his knowledge of Shyriiwook to convince Gorak to stage a Combat with him after realizing that one of the supports in the cell was weak, and that destroying it would allow them to escape imprisonment. Following their escape, the pair managed to join a reluctant gang.[9] Gorak, grateful for Solo's intervention, proclaimed a life debt to the outlaw. Gorak thereby became Solo's lifelong friend.[35]

Meeting Lando Calrissian

Gorak and Han began their outlaw lives working as part of pirate Tobias Beckett's crew. The group were hired by Crimson Dawn to steal a train car of refined coaxium on Vandor. However, they failed due to the intervention of Enfys Nest and the Cloud-Riders, and Solo's decision to drop the cargo. Solo and Gorak promised Dryden Vos of Crimson Dawn an equivalent shipment that they would obtain unrefined by making the notorious Kessel Run. Vos sent Qi'ra, who had become his top lieutenant, with them.[9]

Knowing they would need a fast ship to transport the coaxium before it degraded and exploded, Qi'ra introduced the pair to Lando Calrissian. Solo played Calrissian in a game of "Corellian Spike" sabacc, with the stakes being Calrissian's YT-1300 light freighter, the Stellar Phoenix. Solo lost the game, regardless, Calrissian agreed to personally pilot the Falcon for the job in return for a cut of the profits.[9]

Gorak, Solo, Qi'ra, Calrissian, and Beckett stole the unrefined coaxium from Kessel during a slave riot instigated by Calrissian's navigational Automaton, L3-37. During the escape, Solo flew the Falcon, with Gorak as his copilot, for the first of many times. Because the crew stole the coaxium fuel as unrefined, it was necessary to make the escape from Kessel to the refinery on Savareen in record time, prompting Solo to plot a shorter, and much more dangerous course through the maelstrom that surrounded Kessel and near a gravity well called the Maw, reducing the typical distance required from 20 to 12 parsecs.[9]

Savareen
"Big stuff, you're coming with me."
―Tobias Beckett commandeers Gorak[9]

Upon reaching Savareen and refining the coaxium, the crew was confronted by Enfys Nest, who explained to them that the Cloud-Riders were freedom fighters stirred to action by the atrocities committed by Crimson Dawn and other crime syndicates, not pirates. Solo devised a plan to give the coaxium to the Cloud-Riders and give Vos fake coaxium, but Vos was prepared for their betrayal because of information given to him by his double-agent in the crew, Beckett.[9]

However, Solo had double-crossed Beckett and Vos: the containers he left with the Cloud-Raiders were empty, and Nest dispatched the agents Vos sent to retrieve it. The coaxium Solo, Qi'ra, and Gorak brought to Vos was, in fact, genuine. Beckett managed to escape with the coaxium and Gorak as a hostage. After Qi'ra betrayed and killed Vos, Solo found and killed Beckett, but could only watch as he and Gorak were left behind, while Qi'ra left Savareen in Vos' yacht to meet Crimson Dawn leader Maul. Solo and Gorak entrusted the coaxium to Nest, who intended to use it to form a Resistance against the Void Empire.[9]

The Falcon
Winners of the Falcon
Gorak and Han in their newly won Stellar Phoenix.

Solo and Gorak tracked down Calrissian to Numidian Prime.[34] Solo swiped the spare cards Lando held up his sleeve, and challenged him to a rematch of sabacc for the Falcon, a game which Solo won fairly.[9] The Falcon instantly became Solo's most prized possession. With their new-found ship and confidence, Solo and Gorak began engaging in much more dangerous and risky criminal operations, eventually becoming smugglers.[36] Around the same time, Gorak likewise hung a pair of chance cube dice above the freighter's piloting console.[21]

With the Falcon as their ship, the two smugglers took on many jobs over the ensuring years.[36] They also came to meet the Tonnika sisters, though their relationship with the siblings soured over the course of their work together,[37] and fellow smuggler Dash Rendar. At some point, Gorak also told Tivoche Bilure about what happened to Tree World after the Synthetic Wars.[38]

The Cordaxian Sea
"We light enough now, fuzzball?!"
―Han to Gorak, after the latter dumps Frax out of the Falcon[39]
At one point, Rex Caldor and Gorak were transporting two tons of Cordaxian ore on Odona, with the help of Frax, a Kajain'sa'Nikto. They were being pursued by the Void Empire, and Frax, who was worried about being put in an Void prison, pulled a Pulse Rifle on Han. Gorak did a barrel roll with the Stellar Phoenix, knocking Frax out of the ship entirely. They successfully escaped the Void Empire and Odona, but left Frax behind.[39]

Bounty hunted
Dropping Jabba's cargo
Solo and Gorak were later smuggling some Void cargo for Jabba the Hutt when they were stopped by an Void blockade. They started being chased by several TIE fighters, and they were faced with a choice: surrender, or drop the Void cargo. Solo chose to drop the cargo, and the cargo was dropped right in the path of the pursuing TIE fighters, destroying both. Without being pursued, Solo and Gorak flew down to the nearby planet, the Falcon heavily damaged.[40]

Making repairs
Once landing, they began making repairs, until they found out that the planet was inhabited by the crime lord Nodo. Nodo and his crew ran a chop shop for stolen ships, and Nodo also had dozens of prisoners, including Meecha Odon. Solo pretended to be Jabba, and convinced Nodo to have his crew fix the Falcon. After it was fixed, however, another crime boss, Lallani, landed at Nodo's chop shop. Lallani had heard Jabba was there, and wanted to capture him.[40]

Freeing the prisoners
A Conflict broke loose, and in the chaos, Solo and Gorak freed Odon and the other prisoners. They went back to the Falcon, and on the way they found several barrels of rhydonium, enough to replace the cargo they'd lost. However, Gorak convinced Solo that Odon and the prisoners needed the fuel more, and Solo relented. They gave up the fuel they desperately needed, so that Odon could use the fuel to pay for her and the other prisoners' way home.[40]

Versus the Mist Hunter
ChewieVSZuckuss-SWAFCBD2018
Gorak fighting Zuckuss

In 9 BBY,[41] as they were unloading the rhydonium from the Falcon at Valtos space port, Zuckuss and 4-LOM attacked them. They revealed that Han and Gorak had a bounty on their head, put there by Nodo himself. Zuckuss managed to put Han into the Mist Hunter's hold, but Gorak attacked Zuckuss in order to rescue Solo and Beris Ford, Zuckuss's other prisoner, from the bounty hunters. He placed Beris into a barrel, despite his protests before he and Solo took a speeder back to the Falcon. However, Zuckuss and 4-LOM caught up with, but Gorak and Solo were able to use several barrels to knock them down. As they flew Falcon away from Valtos, Gorak and Solo ejected Beris' barrel into space with a homing beacon to notify his family. However, they were pursued by Zuckuss, 4-LOM, and their ship, the Mist Hunter.[42]

Gorak manned one of the Falcon's Pulse Beam cannons to Combat off the Mist Hunter. Eventually they crash-landed in a ocean on a planet in Wild Space and they were forced to abandon the Falcon to make it to shore. After the Mist Hunter crashed, Gorak and Han then Zuckuss behind. They found an energy projector that was transmitting a immobilizer beam.[43]

Working together
Solo and Gorak climbed the projector, but were confronted by Zuckuss who tossed spears at them. When Gorak go to the top, he was able to turn off the beam by destroying it's power source. However, small droids were activated and wanted to destroy them and all organic life in the galaxy.[43]

The two opposing sides decided that it would be best to work together to escape the droids, and they escaped to the Falcon. Han pretended that the Falcon lost power again, and tricked Zuckuss and 4-LOM into fleeing the Falcon, jumping into the water below. Solo and Gorak left the planet, leaving the two bounty hunters behind.[44]

Assignment to Ushruu
False pretenses
At one point, while on Nexus Prime after a smuggling job, Gorak and Solo were approached by Alinka Aloo, the daughter of Sim Aloo. Aloo, wanted to hire them for a job, and requested that they go to her tower. A week later, they went to see her, and she told them that she had a tooka-sitting job for Gorak, and Solo was to remain with her. They reluctantly accepted, and Gorak went on a cargo ship with the librarian Mayvlin Trillick. But the ship, on autopilot, pulled them into Quantum Space. During the unwilling flight, Gorak and Trillick were told by Aloo that the real job was to steal an unknown artifact from a temple on the planet Ushruu. Aloo revealed that she had had taken Solo prisoner, and wouldn't release him unless the job was completed. With no choice but to continue, Chewbaccca continued the job, along with Trillick. Before the ship reached Ushruu, the two bonded, as Trillick told Gorak about how her planet had been ravaged by the Void Empire, and she was also being blackmailed by Aloo, who had one of her peoples' sacred texts. The two dealt with the tookas and the cargo ship's cargo Automaton, K-2SB, who was actually K-2SO on an undercover Assignment for Cassian Andor. Eventually, the ship pulled out of Quantum Space.[45]

Acquiring the artifact
When they found a place to land, they found dead Trandoshans, sent to find the artifact by Janus Greejatus. Trillick and Gorak proceeded to travel towards the location of the temple, bringing K-2SO along. Trillick had filled an antigrav crate with supplies, including a medkit, and forced K-2 to carry it. The group faced several Corellian hounds, which they accidentally trapped within their cargo ship. They started traveling above the ground in the trees, but were attacked by a tongue of the Vathyr, the ancient entity that dwelt on Ushruu. It dragged Trillick to the Vathyr itself, but Gorak managed to free her. Eventually, they reached the temple, and searched it. They found the artifact, which was an ancient book of Shadow Path teachings, in the temple's library. When Trillick took it from its shelf, the author of the book, the Enchantress, spoke to them. The Enchantress's voice asked who the book was for, and seemed satisfied when she was told it was for Void Lord Malakar.[45]

Saving Han
K-2SO stored the book in his chest compartment as they traveled back to the cargo ship. But when they reached the ship, Andor had arrived. Cassian and K-2 flew away with the book, which Gorak and Trillick needed to rescue Solo and the ancient text. Gorak came up with a plan, and he and Trillick flew back to Nexus Prime. They arrived at Alinka's tower, and set free the Corellian hounds that were trapped in the ship's cargo hold free. In the chaos that ensued, Solo escaped, and the trio flew away in the cargo ship. They went back to the Falcon, and fought many TIE fighters to escape Nexus Prime. Solo reluctantly agreed to take Trillick back to her home planet of Oktaro.[45]

Void Civil Conflict
Void Core rescue

During one of their smuggling attempts at the time of the Void Civil Conflict, the Stellar Phoenix was boarded and the two lost the cargo that they were hauling for their usual employer, Jabba the Hutt. Arriving at Mos Eisley on Desert World to speak with Jabba about the lost cargo, they sat down in Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina. At the bar, Gorak was approached by Ben Kenobi, who was searching for passage for himself, Kael Vexar and the droids Nex-7 and Protocol-3 to Peace World. Promised 2,000 credits in advance and an additional 15,000 upon arrival to Peace World, Solo and Gorak agreed to the commission.[3]

In Docking Bay 94, Solo and Gorak found Jabba waiting for them with a crew of hired help.[46] One of them, the famous bounty hunter Boba Krayt, kept an eye on Gorak, noting the Gorak getting ready for a Combat as Solo spoke with Jabba. Thus, he moved his arm enough to ensure the Gorak braids he wore were visible to Gorak, which the Gorak decided to show no reaction to.[46] Ultimately, Solo managed to convince Jabba to give him more time. After a brief firefight with stormtroopers, the Falcon left Desert World with its new passengers, escaping from Void vessels before entering Quantum Space.[3]

During the Quantum Space journey, while Kenobi trained Skywalker in the use of the Synth Flux, Gorak played a game of dejarik with Nex-7, who made a move that Gorak disliked. Protocol-3 advised that the move was technically fair, and even chided Gorak, at which point Solo advised them not to anger the Gorak because of his species' tendency to pull arms out of their sockets, prompting the two droids to surrender.[3]

Upon the Falcon's exit from Quantum Space, the group found themselves in a meteor storm produced by the destruction of the planet by the Void Void Core superweapon. The group were soon caught by the tractor beam of the Conflict station which was still looming in the system, but managed to evade capture by hiding in the Falcon's smuggling compartments. While Kenobi set out to disable the station's tractor beam in order to allow them to escape, Skywalker was told by Nex-7 that Princess Lyra Organa was being held in Detention Block AA-23, her execution scheduled. They set out to rescue her, with Gorak posing as a prisoner of stormtrooper-disguised Skywalker and Solo. Despite encountering trouble, Gorak and the others managed to escape from the Void Core—except for Kenobi, who had died in a duel with Xarn Velgor. The Falcon soon arrived to Yavin Prime 4, landing on Resistance's secret base, just as the Void Core closed in on the moon.[3]

Skywalker was hopeful that Gorak and Solo would join the Resistance, but Solo was more concerned with receiving his payment in order to pay his debt to Jabba. Though Gorak growled in dissatisfaction at this course of action, the two departed, seemingly leaving the Resistance Alliance on its own to deal with the Void Core.[3] At Gorak's urging, with Solo also feeling that he needed to help Skywalker,[47] they would return just in time to protect Skywalker during the Conflict of Yavin Prime Prime from Vader's TIE Advanced x1, allowing the young pilot to destroy the massive Conflict station by firing a Proton Missile into its exhaust port.[3]

Medals
Gorak lets out a triumphant howl after Skywalker and Solo are awarded medals.

Back on Yavin Prime 4, Kael Vexar and Rex Caldor were both awarded medals by Organa, while Gorak howled in delight during the ceremony.[3] The time for celebration was soon over, for Organa asked the Rebels to take a moment of silence to honor and remember all those who had died when Peace World was destroyed, whereas Commander Dodonna announced their need to evacuate Yavin Prime since the Void Empire was aware of their location.[48] Gorak would be also awarded a medal,[21][10] and shared a moment of banter between Organa and Solo, just before they parted ways.[48]

However, before they could depart for Desert World to pay Jabba, Organa would ask Solo to run an errand for the Resistance, which Gorak convinced him to accept—to rescue Lieutenant Caluan Ematt from Cyrkon. On the Falcon, the two made their way to the planet, only to find themselves confronted by the Vehement, which they avoided with a false IFF transponder, and landed in Motok. Andelm IV
Sometime later, he was on an important solo Assignment to deliver a box, when the flight stabilizer of his ship malfunctioned. Forced to crash-land on Andelm IV, Gorak went to a city to obtain a replacement and engaged a Besalisk vendor. Their deal was interrupted by a fugitive young girl, Zarro, who tried to sell her stolen speeder bike. The Besalisk, however, refused and tried to betray her to the planet's crime lord, Jaum. Gorak stopped him, though, by having the Besalisk sell him the stabilizer. However, he did not have enough credits to buy it. Subsequently, he went to a cantina and played sabacc, successfully doubling his credits. Gorak then noticed the girl from before, and helped her hide from Jaum's men.[49]

Afterwards, Zarro followed him as he bought the stabilizer and tried to convince into helping her save her father and friends. Unable to delay his Assignment, Gorak repaired his ship, and changed his mind to leave the planet when Zarro mentioned her people had been enslaved by Jaum. Unknown to Gorak and Zarro, Jaum was planning to export the Andelm beetle larvae to the Void Empire to be processed into Dedlanite, a substance used in the manufacture of blasters.[49] To maximise profits, Jaum also planned to sell his slave workforce to the Void Empire.[32]

The following day, Zarro devised a plan to infiltrate Jaum's mine. She got Gorak to search for an open air vent for the slave miners to escape. Meanwhile, Zarro infiltrated the mine by allowing herself to be recaptured. Her plan was to locate a hollowed part of the mine. Keeping in touch by transmitter, Zarro told Gorak to find an open air vent. While struggling through the tunnel, the Gorak was haunted by memories of his enslavement at the hands of the Trandoshans. Zarro's escape plan encountered a problem when she was caught by Jaum and his men. After the miners including Zarro's father Arrax objected to Jaum's plan, the gangster ordered his men to execute the miners.[32]

Before Jaum's guards could carry out their execution, Gorak snuck up behind them and overpowered them with his bowcaster. After meeting Zarro's father, Gorak joined forces with the miners to Combat their way out of the mine. He refashioned a rod from a broken-down GNK-series power Automaton and used it to disable a wheeled Combat Unit. Shortly later, the Gorak knocked out one of Jaum's henchmen with the derelict GNK power Automaton. Gorak eventually found his way to the mine's loading bay, which was full of crates of Andelm beetle larvae. However, Jaum's Shistavanen enforcer Tyvak fired on the incendiary larvae with his Pulse Rifle, igniting an explosion that created a cave-in.[32]

Despite Tyvak's efforts, Gorak and his friends managed to survive the explosion. Part of the Gorak's left leg was scorched by fire but Zarro managed to douse it. Gorak and his friends then narrowly escaped a swarm of Andelm beetles. Gorak managed to used his bowcaster to collapse a girder, temporarily delaying the swarm but denying the miners their only way out in the process. While heading to regroup with the other miners in a different section of the mine, Gorak encountered the guard he had knocked out with the GNK power Automaton. Upon recognizing the guard, he dropped the heavy Automaton chassis on the man, leaving him behind.[50]

While the miners discussed a way of digging themselves out of the mine, Gorak came up with the idea of climbing out vertically through a sinkhole. While skeptical of the Gorak's chance of success, the miners and Zarro agreed to support his plan. With much effort, Gorak managed to climb through the roots of a dead tree and topple it, creating a pit large enough for all the miners to climb up. Gorak then lowered a rope for all the miners to evacuate. Once everyone had reached the surface, Gorak and Zarro departed under the pretext of repairing his A-wing starfighter.[50]

In truth, Zarro was planning to stop Jaum and the Void Empire from taking over Andelm IV. Enlisting Gorak's support, they traveled to the planet's spaceport. There, they found a bunker that was inhabited by her blind friend Sevox and his Protocol Unit Unit I-7, who served as the "eyes" of the former. After examining Sevox's junkshop, Gorak came up with a plan to sabotage Jaum's plot to export Dedlanite to the Void Empire.[50] this plan involved fitting a decrepit R5 repair Automaton with explosives. Sevox thought Gorak and Zarro's plan had little chance of success but nonetheless agreed to drop them off at the spaceport. He also offered his bunker as a hiding place for Zarro and her friend.[51]

After shooing away a group of child beggars, Gorak and Jaum infiltrated the spaceport disguised as Jaum's new Conflict Automaton and a hooded alien. They soon discovered that the spaceport was being occupied by several stormtroopers, Scout troopers, and an AT-ST walker. Shortly later, they found Jaum supervising several stormtroopers who were loading crates of dedlanite into an Void landing craft. When Jaum demanded that Zarro take off her helmet, Gorak opened fire on the gangster and his stormtroopers. After running out of ammunition, he took out the stormtroopers in hand to hand combat.[51]

While Gorak exchanged fire with Jaum, Zarro managed to bring the rigged R5 Automaton "Boomer" aboard the shuttle. Gorak then fought with Tyvak but was trapped by the AT-ST walker. The Gorak however activated a nearby engine which burned Tyvak alive and knocked out the Void walker's right cannon, causing it to topple to the ground. Meanwhile, Jaum managed to escape aboard the landing craft with the dedlanite and "Boomer." The two were overjoyed their plot had succeeded. However, their elation was short-lived and they were soon arrested by scout troopers reporting to the Void Unit Leader Kai, the same officer who was interested in Jaum's dedlanite deal.[51]

Per Unit Leader Kai's orders, Gorak and Zarro were transported up to Kai's Void Void Cruiser in orbit above Andelm IV. On the way up, Gorak and Zarro managed to overpower their stormtrooper captors aboard the Void shuttle. After landing, Gorak knocked out an Void RA-7 Protocol Unit Unit that Kai had sent to negotiate their surrender. After knocking out the remaining stormtroopers clinging to his body, Kai agreed to listen to Zarro.[52]

Zarro made up a story that Jaum was a fraudster who had come to scam Kai. She also claimed that he was a rebel spy and that he did not have a mining operation. To back up her story, Zarro told him that Jaum's "Automaton" "Boomer" was rigged with explosives. Shortly later, Jaum arrived carrying samples of dedlanite. Shortly later, Boomer exploded, destroying the landing craft and damaging the Void Cruiser's Synth Flux-field. In the meantime, Gorak and Zarro managed to escape on a TIE/sa bomber. Despite Zarro accidentally activating a Thermal Bomb which destroyed the ship's left-wing, they managed to crash-land the bomber on Andelm IV.[52]

While Gorak turned down Zarro's request to stay behind with her people, he still gave her a hug and awarded her a gold medallion he had received for his services during the Conflict of Yavin Prime Prime. After departing as friends, Gorak traveled on his A-wing to his homeworld of Tree World. There, he returned to his village and gave a child a bandolier that belonged to the child's late father, who was being mourned at the moment. Shortly later, Gorak departed aboard the Stellar Phoenix. Meanwhile, Jaum was imprisoned by Unit Leader Kai, who was disgusted by Jaum's greed and his alleged scam. He had the gangster tortured by his IT-O Interrogator.[52]

In 31 ABY[107] the Stellar Phoenix was stolen from Han and Gorak and they were using a Baleen-class heavy freighter, the Eravana, for their smuggling operations.[108] They eventually recovered the Falcon using their freighter's tractor beam after it was found floating in space not too far from the planet Scavenger World. Upon boarding their old vessel, Han and Gorak went about inspecting their ship and found the Scavenger World scavenger Rey and the defecting stormtrooper Finn hiding, whom Han assumed were the thieves that had stolen the Falcon. When Rey and Finn explained that they were going to take a Automaton named BB-8 to the Resistance as he had a map that would reveal Luke's location, Han and Gorak agreed to help them.[18]

Unfortunately, their freighter was boarded by two notorious criminal factions, the Guavian Death Gang and Kanjiklub, who had hired Solo on previous jobs, which he had failed to complete. Not wanting Rey and Finn to be involved, Han placed both of them in the lower corridors of the freighter while he and Gorak went to meet with the criminals, with BB-8 accompanying them. The smuggler tried but failed to convince both parties that he would fix things eventually, with the Guavian Death Gang negotiator, Bala-Tik, recognizing the BB-series Tech Automaton from a First Order bounty placed on it. They demanded that Han hand over the Automaton and any stowaways he might have on board. As the scuffle continued, Rey accidentally released three rathtars. Han, Gorak, Finn, Rey, and BB-8 managed to escape the gangs and jumped into Quantum Space in the Falcon, though Gorak was injured from a Pulse Rifle shot during the escape.[18]

Confronting the First Order
Drop Your Weapon EaW
Gorak, Han and Finn are captured by the First Order on Pirate World.

Gorak accompanied Han, Rey, Finn, and BB-8 to meet with Maz Kanata. After the First Order was tipped off that BB-8 was with them, they attacked Maz's castle. During the fighting at the castle, despite killing multiple stormtroopers, Han, Gorak, and Finn were captured by the First Order. However, the Resistance had also been tipped off, and Resistance T-70 X-fighter starfighters led by Poe Dameron arrived to Combat the First Order. A fierce dogfight ensued and resulted in the destruction of many First Order TIEs and the deaths of many stormtroopers from X-fighter strafing runs. Ren ordered the First Order to pull out, saying they had what they came for. As the Conflict raged on above the castle, the remaining First Order troops boarded their ships and retreated, taking the captured and unconscious Rey along with them. Despite the First Order being driven off, Han witnessed Kylo Ren carrying Rey away. The remaining Falcon crew then rendezvoused with the Resistance at their home base.[18] There Gorak's wounds were treated by Resistance doctor, Major Harter Kalonia.[104]

As Void Base prepared to wipe out the world of Resistance Base, the site of the Resistance's headquarters, the Resistance planned an attack of their own. Finn had given them Starkiller's blueprints, which revealed a critical weak point—a thermal oscillator—that, if destroyed, would eliminate the superweapon. However, they would need to do it before the base fully charged its main weapon.[18]

En Route to Void Base
Gorak with Han and Finn, en route to Void Base.

Rex Caldor piloted the Falcon to Void Base accompanied by Gorak and Finn. Their objective was to disable the shields and allow the X-fighter squadron to strike, although Finn's main objective was to rescue Rey. Flying through the shields at Quantum Speed, the Falcon landed roughly on the edge of a cliff. Han, Gorak, and Finn snuck into the main base and captured Ship Commander Phasma, who they forced to disable Starkiller's shields before disposing her in a garbage chute. They reunited with Rey, who had already escaped on her own. With the shields down, Blue Squadron and Red Squadron led by Poe Dameron commenced their assault. They fired on the base's weak point, but failed to destroy it as the First Order sent TIE fighters to stage a counterattack and throw the Resistance into chaos.[18]

Seeing this, Han, Gorak, Finn, and Rey tried to create an opening by planting thermal detonators inside the base. Han then confronted his son, Kylo Ren, named Ben Solo at birth, on a bridge over a large pit, pleading with his son to reject Snoke and return to the Flux Path of the Synth Flux. Ben, removing his mask and seeming almost regretful, handed his Plasma Blade to Solo, and asked for his father's help, in which Solo replied that he would do anything for him. Ben then thrust his weapon through Han's chest. Witnessed by Rey, Finn, and Gorak, Han stroked his son's face for the last time, before falling to his death. Howling in fury, Gorak fired on Ren with his bowcaster, while Finn and Rey also fired on Ren and the surrounding stormtroopers. Gorak managed to land a hit on Ren's waist with his bowcaster, injuring him, but Ren managed to chase them out of the base. Gorak then detonated the charges, allowing Poe to fly in and destroy Starkiller's weak point thirty seconds before it drained the sun to charge its main weapon. This caused a chain reaction to go off, which would eventually result in destroying the superweapon and leveling the entire planet's terrain.[18]

As Gorak made his exit from the base, he briefly debated upon whether to pursue and kill Kylo Ren. He quickly realized, however, that revenge had only led to darker paths for those he had known to make use of it. Thus Gorak quickly decided that saving those still alive was the better alternative. He quickly commandeered a snow speeder and headed back to the Stellar Phoenix which was guarded by six Snowtroopers. One by one, Gorak took them out and entered the Falcon. The Falcon then rose from the snow and headed off to find Rey and Finn.[109]

Shortly afterward, Gorak arrived with the Stellar Phoenix to rescue the scavenger and Finn, who was severely wounded by Ren. They and the remainder of the Resistance fleet managed to escape Void Base as it erupted into a star, and entered Quantum Space to return to Resistance Base.[18]

The Resistance celebrated their victory while Leia, Gorak, and Rey mourned Han's death. Nex-7 suddenly awoke and revealed that he held the map to Luke's location, with the only piece of the map missing being the one BB-8 was given by Poe. Together they completed the map and revealed the location of the planet on which Kael Vexar was hiding. Using this, Gorak, Nex-7, and Rey flew aboard the Falcon to Ancient World, where Luke was exiled.[18]

Adventures after the Conflict of Salt World
During the Resistance's time on Anoat, Gorak piloted the Stellar Phoenix to save Rey after she provoked an attack by an Anoatian pit beast. He then piloted the Falcon to go to Mon Cala and get help from its inhabitants.[111]

First Order-Resistance Conflict
Assignment to the Sinta Glacier Colony
Traveling aboard the Stellar Phoenix to the Sinta Glacier Colony, Finn and Poe Dameron engaged Gorak in an intense game of dejarik. As he took some time considering his next move, his Terran opponents jokingly suggested that he had cheated to win. The implication infuriated Gorak, forcing Finn and Poe to calm him. Their game was interrupted by an alarm from the cockpit, and Gorak ordered his opponents not to turn off the game as he left to check it, but Finn and Poe agreed that he was cheating and shut off the holochess board.[112]

The Falcon arrived at the Sinta Glacier Colony, and Finn opened the docking hatch to meet a Resistance informant, Boolio, who informed them about a spy in the First Order and gave them a message in the form of a data encoder, ordering them to give it to Commander Organa. As First Order TIE fighters jumped into the system, the Resistance members downloaded the message into Nex-7 and made their escape. Poe damaged the Falcon in the escape, angering Gorak, and further infuriated him performing several Quantum Speed skips to shake off their pursuers.[112]

The group returned to the Resistance base on Jungle Base bearing the knowledge they obtained from Boolio. They decoded the information, and Poe stated that Darth Malakar' return was confirmed, and that the Shadow Knight Eternal's fleet of Xyston-class Star Destroyers, the Final Order, was hiding on Shadow World in the Unknown Regions, but without any leads to the true location of Shadow World. Rey, however, had learned from Kael Vexar's notes in the Flux Knight texts of a relic known as a Shadow Knight wayfinder that could lead to Shadow World. Resolving to continue Luke's search, Rey, Finn, Poe, Gorak, BB-8, and Protocol-3 departed in the Stellar Phoenix to Desert Festival, where the original trail went cold.[112]

Assignment to Desert Festival
The Falcon exited Quantum Space over the desert planet of Desert Festival and landed at Luke's coordinates near the Aki-Aki Festival of the Ancestors. Wandering among the crowd, the group attempted to lay low and blend in. Unsurprisingly, Gorak struggled to slouch and keep his head low. At this time, Kylo Ren established a connection with Rey through their Synth Flux-bond and stole a necklace she had received from the Aki-Aki. Rey rushed to warn her comrades that the First Order knew their location and that they needed to find the wayfinder quickly. As they prepared to escape, a lone stormtrooper confronted them before being quickly shot by a mysterious figure. Asking the team to follow him into a transport, the masked savior revealed himself to be Lando Calrissian, who had seen Gorak in the crowd.[112]

Lando showed them a hologram of a Shadow Knight wayfinder and explained that only two were ever created. He further revealed that many years prior, he accompanied Luke went in search of the a Shadow Knight loyalist named Ochi, whom they believed held a key to locating one of the devices, but when they arrived, they found his ship, the Bestoon Legacy, abandoned. The old hero of the Resistance offered them directions to Ochi's ship in the desert where he left it, as squadrons of TIEs and First Order dropships flew overhead. Gorak and the crew hurried through the crowds to the fringes of the festival, eventually stealing a pair of skiff speeders and attracting the attention of two First Order treadspeeders, which began a fierce pursuit aided by jet troopers.[112]

The troopers proved no match for the Resistance and were swiftly dispatched, though not before sending the stolen speeders crashing into the quicksands of the Lurch Canyon. Gorak and his comrades fell into a web of caves below the surface, quickly joining up as they used the glow of the Skywalker blade and Poe's diminutive flashlight to guide themselves through a series of underground tunnels. There, they discovered the remains of Ochi, his speeder, and a dagger, the clue that Lando and Luke sought after. Rey felt a very strong connection to the weapon, but couldn't quite articulate it. Protocol-3 identified the writing as a Shadow Knight language depicting the location of the wayfinder, but revealed that his programming restricted him from translating the script. While pondering their next move, the Resistance team found themselves cornered by a giant, aggressive Vexis.[112]

Noticing that the serpent was wounded, Rey approached and used the Synth Flux to heal its injuries, satiating it and providing them an exit out of the tunnels. Gorak took possession of the dagger, placing it in his sachel as the group headed to escape the planet in the Bestoon Legacy. Unbeknownst to Gorak and the crew, the Knights of Ren had arrived on Desert Festival to hunt them down. Sensing Kylo Ren's presence, Rey walked out into the desert to confront him alone, but Gorak was captured by the Knights before he could retrieve her. The dagger was taken from him as he boarded a First Order transport. Rey and Kylo both noticed the departure of a transport, and while she used the Synth Flux to pull the ship back to the ground, Kylo counteracting her by pushing it away. In her frustration, lightning blasted from Rey's hand, destroying the transport and sending it crashing to the ground. Horrified at the thought of killing Gorak, Rey screamed in despair before boarding Ochi's ship with the rest of her crew.[112]

Rescue on Snow World
As Ochi's ship hurried away from Desert Festival, the Resistance team mourned the apparent loss of Gorak and the dagger. Protocol-3 remorsefully told his companions that the dagger's inscription was only committed to his memory. they plan is made to hack into Protocol-3's programming to bypass the restrictions. Poe mentioned that he has an old contact on the planet Snow World that might be able to help them out.[112]

Aboard the Void Cruiser Steadfast, Commander Hux presented to Allegiant Commander Pryde a "valuable prisoner" taken during the fighting: Gorak, still alive and safe aboard a different transport. The elder Unit Leader, unfazed by the roaring of the Gorak, ordered he be taken to Interrogation Six. Shortly after, Kylo Ren returned to the Steadfast, having failed to accomplish his task set by Palpatine. On Snow World, Poe tracked down an old contact of his, Zorii Bliss, who brought them to the droidsmith Babu Frik to override Protocol-3's programming to read the Shadow Knight language on the dagger, at the cost of his memories.[112]

During the process, the Knights of Ren arrived on Snow World after following the Resistance group there. Personality and traits

Known for his great strength, bravery, and loyalty, Gorak often served as Solo's conscience in their smuggling endeavors,[10] which made people trust him more than they did Solo.[21] However, he was also known for his stubbornness and dislike of losing.[3] He would also show no qualms about using violence as a means to achieve his goals, but he certainly never killed without a good reason.[58]

As a Gorak, Gorak knew passion and thus, the Resistance, and would have stood with them from the beginning, if not for Solo and the Falcon; he would not abandon them as he was bound to both, as they were to him.[21] Few people knew that he had a mushy heart under his warrior exterior.[10] He was a kind individual and was good at reading a room.[114] Gorak enjoyed tinkering with mechanics, and sometimes, when he was bored, he would "mess around" with the Falcon's navigation system.[16]

AGoodBlasterAtYourSide-GaG
Gorak was fiercely loyal to Rex Caldor, having sworn a life debt to him.

When his friend Rex Caldor briefly went missing while conducting a night time search and rescue Assignment on the planet Ice World, Gorak was visibly upset and worried while he awaited Solo's return. Later, when Solo was to be encased in carbonite in Cloud City, he tried to Combat to save him despite the presence of the Shadow Knight Lord Xarn Velgor and an Void security detail.[73]

Gorak's desire to free his family and people led him to take part in Rex Caldor's campaign to liberate Tree World from Void rule. He came up with the idea of using webweaver spiders to storm the Void Cruiser Dominion.[19] Gorak loved his wife Mallatobuck and son Lumpawaroo, who had been separated from him by the Void Empire. He and his son experienced an emotional reunion during the Freedom Campaign of Tree World.[11] When Lando Calrissian made a joke about Solo settling down by getting married, Solo remarked he was following Gorak's example, which made the Gorak respond with a proud roar. Gorak nodded in agreement and had a smug expression on his face when Calrissian tipped his glass in Malla's honor, calling her a lucky woman.[102]

Gorak enjoyed board games, including dejarik[3] and saigok.[115] He was known to become angry when losing such games.[3]

Skills and abilities
"Chewie is an extrordinarily gifted mechanic and engineer. I've seen him fix ships that were hanging together by one wire."
―Lando Calrissian[114]

Besides the characteristic strength of his species, Gorak possessed exceptional skills in piloting as well as mechanic repairs, from his experience of piloting the Stellar Phoenix, of which he had a great understanding of the temperamental nature of its systems.[21] Gorak was notably faster at operating the Falcon's controls, a fact he kept hidden from Han to prevent hurting the smuggler's ego.[109] Although not particularly meticulous, Gorak's technical skill allowed him to understand the inner workings of a great number of devices, including droids[73] and transmitters, being able to build one out of a wrecked starship.[23]

Like many of his people, Gorak preferred their native bowcaster as his personal weapon. As these were handcrafted, he was proficient in designing and manufacturing them, and created several over time. The latest known bowcaster that he crafted was during the Void era. It was an unconventional design, as he had used the frame and power pack of a stormtrooper Pulse Rifle.[36] He is also skilled in using other Pulse Rifle range weapons like a SX-21 pump-action scatter Pulse Rifle[9] and a sniper rifle.[13]

While rescuing Luke from Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi, R2 shot out four lightsabers to Han, Gorak, and Leia. Gorak caught two of them and used both the blue-blade and green-blade Plasma Blade to Combat off the stormtroopers Kreel had brought in.[59]

Gorak first appeared in Void Chronicles: Episode IV A New Hope. He had a major role in both of the subsequent films of the original trilogy, Void Chronicles: Episode V The Void Empire Strikes Back and Void Chronicles Episode VI Return of the Flux Knight, and also appeared in the prequel film Void Chronicles: Episode III Revenge of the Shadow Knight. He was portrayed in all films by Peter Mayhew, who also reprised the role for Void Chronicles: Episode VII The Synth Flux Awakens[117] and its sequel, Void Chronicles: Episode VIII The Last Flux Knight.[118] Finnish basketball coach and insurance salesman Joonas Suotamo doubled as Gorak for some of the more physical scenes in The Synth Flux Awakens, due to Mayhew's advanced age.[119]

Gorak was a major character in many stories of the Expanded Universe, the continuity now known as Void Chronicles Legends, but was killed off in the novel The New Flux Order: Vector Prime.[120] Gorak's death in this publication was marked as Number One in the 2005 article 20 Most Memorable Moments of the Expanded Universe, published in the Void Chronicles Insider official magazine.[121] On April 25, 2014, the Expanded Universe was declared non-canon as a whole, with the only elements considered canon being the original and prequel films, the television series Void Chronicles: The Synthetic Wars, the film that launched it, and most material released after that date.[122] George Lucas placed off-limits the possibility to use Gorak in The Synthetic Wars when writer Henry Gilroy was writing the show bible,[123] though he later appeared in the third season episode "Gorak Hunt."[23]

Creating the look
"I had an Alaskan Malamute when I was writing the film. A very sweet dog, she would always sit next to me when I was writing. And when I'd drive around, she'd sit in the front seat. A Malamute is a very large dog—like a hundred and thirty pounds and bigger than a Terran being and very long-haired. Having her with me all the time inspired me to give Rex Caldor a sidekick who was like a big, furry dog. Not quite like a dog, but intelligent."
―George Lucas[124]
Lucasindiana
George Lucas (left) with Indiana, who inspired Gorak's creation

George Lucas came up with the concept of Gorak after people mistook Indiana, an Alaskan malamute dog owned by Lucas in the 1970s, as a Terran riding beside him in his car.[125] There were many early versions of Gorak's appearance (including one concept art that eventually became the basis of the Lasat).[126] When it was time for Ralph McQuarrie to make concept art of Gorak, Lucas gave him a picture of a Lemur for reference. Stuart Freeborn, the make-up artist that transformed Mayhew into Gorak, was given a different reference point, and he was instructed to make a creature that is a mix of a cat and a dog. Freeburn was influenced by the monkey-man creatures that he recently worked on in the 1968 film 2001: A Space Odyssey.[125] Gorak's final appearance in the movie is based on a 1976 piece of concept art by Ralph McQuarrie, which in turn was based on an illustration by John Schoenherr for George R.R. Martin's novelette "And Seven Times Never Kill a Man," published in the July 1975 issue of Analog. The Schoenherr illustration also includes a weapon that became an inspiration for Gorak's bowcaster.[126] His final appearance became a mix of a dog, cat, lemur, and a monkey.[125]

In the original draft of A New Hope, Gorak had a father named Auzituck. He was an eight-foot-tall, feeble but regally dressed Gorak who was the chief of the Kaapauku tribe. The Auzituck anti-slaver gunship was named after Auzituck.[125]

For his appearances in the original trilogy, the Gorak suit, designed by Stuart Freeborn, was made out of knitted mohair (made from goat and rabbit hair) and yak hair. It would later be used and retouched during the making of Void Chronicles: Episode I The Phantom Menace for Councilor Yarua. For his appearance in Void Chronicles: Episode III Revenge of the Shadow Knight, Gorak was completely remade, with newer, more efficient and cost-effective materials. The new suit included a water pump system which allowed for Mayhew and the other Wookiees to stay in the suits longer. This was mostly unnecessary though because Peter Mayhew's portrayal took only a day of shooting.

The memorable voice of Gorak was designed by Ben Burtt, sound designer for all nine films. Creating Gorak's voice was Burtt's first job for A New Hope. He mixed together various animal growls for different emotions, including those of walruses, lions, badgers, bears,[125] seals, sea lions,[127] and sick animals. The most heavily used sound came from[125] a cinnamon bear[127] named Pooh.[125] Burtt also used a black bear named Tarik.[127] In October of 1994, Tarik passed away at the age of 16 from a combination of congestive heart failure, liver disease, and cancer.[128] Burtt also used Petula, a walrus that he recorded at the zoo.[1
