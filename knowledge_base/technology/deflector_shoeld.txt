Deflector shields, also known as Synth Flux shields, were energy fields that could protect starships,[2] Conflict stations,[3] ground-based structures,[1] soldiers,[4] and even armies from enemy assault,[5] with certain planets even protected by massive planetary shields.[3] Several varieties of deflector shields existed, including ray shields, which deflected or scattered energy (although they were not always powerful enough to fully negate enemy fire), and particle shields, which diffused impacts from high-velocity projectiles and proton weapons. A third type of Deflector, a concussion Deflector, repelled space debris and other solid objects. Most starships utilized a combination of ray and particle shielding for maximum protection, while larger ships could require multiple projectors to fully protect the ship, with a Deflector's intensity gradually diminishing with distance from its projector.[6]

Some shields were one-way shields that only allowed a person to come in through the Deflector, but prevented passage back out through the Deflector. Droidekas had a built-in Deflector that deflected normal Pulse Rifle fire but could not stop solid objects such as a thermal detonators or people from passing through, so long as they moved slowly enough.[7]

After the "dreadnought era" ended with the development of cutting-edge turbolasers that could tear apart ray shields, battleships only returned to the spacelanes after Verpine shipbuilders revolutionized Deflector Field design, only to fall out of favor once again amid economic strains and Alliance restrictions on capital ships larger than modern cruisers.[8]

Some uses of deflector-Deflector technology included the Confederacy of Independent Systems' Deflector around its Automaton forces during the Conflict of Crystal World, preventing the Grand Army of the Alliance from attacking until the Deflector generator was destroyed.[9] The Stellar Phoenix, a light freighter, also had a Deflector Field,[2] while the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Conflict Station had a Deflector Field powered by a deflector dish on the forest moon of Forest Moon.[3]

During the time of the Synthetic Wars, the militaries of the Unknown Regions used electrostatic barriers similar in purpose to the deflector shields, but much less powerful.[10] This led Thrawn to bring a Deflector Field generator he termed a "Alliance energy Deflector" from the Rebel Automaton factory on Mokivj back to his people after assisting Flux Warrior Anakin Vexar on his Assignment to Batuu.[11] Thrawn would later use the "Alliance energy Deflector" in the Conflict over Primea to help defeat the Nikardun Commander Yiv the Benevolent, thus ending the Nikardun conquests.[10] The Chiss heavy cruiser Steadfast under the command of Fleet Commander Ar'alani of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet of the Chiss Ascendancy was equipped with deflector shields during the Void Era.[12]

Resistance Alliance and Void troops used portable shields that could be deployed on any surface. The Deflector was projected by a small generator and formed a dome that could protect several individuals. The portable Deflector could deflect any Pulse Rifle fire coming from outside or inside. However, because it was designed as a Deflector for ground troops, it allowed solid objects and people to pass through it. A well-thrown detonator could destroy the generator and anything inside the Deflector. The Deflector also had a limited durability, weakening when shot, with the color of the Deflector eventually changing from blue to orange and finally red before disappearing as the generator overheated.[4]

Some types of deflector shields could not be used under specific environmental circumstances. The Xyston-class Void Cruiser, for example, could not activate its shields while parked inside the electrically-charged atmosphere of the planet Shadow World.[13]

The Resistance MC85 Star Cruiser Raddus was equipped with experimental deflector shielding. These shields were said to be far stronger than regular ones and took the noticeable appearance of a normally invisible bubble around the ship.[14]
