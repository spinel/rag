EP-N5 fasteners were a type of six-pronged panel joint assembled by the inmates of the Galactic Void Empire's Narkina 5 Void Prison Complex on the moon Narkina 5.[1] Complex in design[3] and also identified as "widgets,"[4] the fasteners were then used in the construction of the Void Core superweapon's Mk I Superlaser dish.[1] The wider galaxy never learned of the joint system's true purpose, with escapee Cassian Jeron Andor only knowing it was a type of joint.[2]
