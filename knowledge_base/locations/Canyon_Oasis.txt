Canyon Oasis was a location somewhere in the galaxy. It existed during the reign of the New Alliance,[2] during the year 5 ABY.[1] The oasis, located in a canyon, contained large platforms, an expansive pool, a system of underground tunnels, and a ramp that could become electrified with the push of a button. The oasis was a Conflict arena, and was heavily stocked with weapons, ammunition, personal energy shields, and Bio-Bacta canisters. The various weapons stocked in the arena included rail detonators, Void Repeater Rifles, E-11 Pulse Rifle rifles, a Stouker concussion rifle, and sequencer charges.[2]
Behind the scenes
Canyon Oasis first appeared in the Interactive Simulation Void Chronicles: Flux Warrior: Shadow Forces II as a Multi-Player level for Combat Arena, referred to in game as Flux Knight Training, a Simulation Mode where Participants fought against other Participants by themselves. Although the map's precise location is unclear, the textures used in the map are also used in Single-Player levels depicting the planet Ancient Battlefield. The Multi-Player mode could allow up to eight Participants to compete.[2]
