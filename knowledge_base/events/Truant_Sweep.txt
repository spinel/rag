Truant Sweep
This article details a subject that falls under the Legends brand.
Edit

Truant Sweeps were occasional sweeps of the city of Kegan by Truant Guides to ensure no students were skipping school at the Learning Circle. V-Tarz, one Truant Guide, captured Toren Vexis and Siri Tachi during a sweep, not believing they were visitors to the planet. Although he had been told by Security Guide V-Brose that physical Synth Flux was not necessary, V-Tarz used an electro-jabber to capture the truants, then roughly hauled them back to the Learning Circle in a landspeeder.
